Quando crei un account (con email + password oppure con Google), l'app salva su Firebase questi dati:

  • a public nickname (you choose it) and an avatar
  • your game progress: streaks, accuracy percentage, per-script statistics, daily challenge history

Profile data (nickname, avatar, statistics, unlocked badges) is publicly visible on the app's /u/:nickname page and on the leaderboard. Email and friend list stay private: only you can see them.

If you use the app as a guest, nothing leaves your device. Preferences (language, theme, sounds) and game progress are saved only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.

The app uses two Google services:
